How to read the numbers

  • A level is a score that only a small share of healthy drives ever reach. “Healthy drives flagged” is that share, over about five months.
  • “Failed drives detected” counts only failed drives that had a valid 14-day window (189 of 274).
  • The risk score ranks drives, it is not a probability of failure.
  • The anomaly model never saw failures. It is a weak failure detector and is shown separately.
